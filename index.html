Forts are intrinsically linked to the Rajput relation with military defense, consolidation of power, and cultural identity. As a warrior clan, the Rajputs built numerous magnificent forts for defense, and they also served as symbols of their power, administrative centers, and places of refuge for their people. Examples include Chittorgarh Fort, Amber Fort, and Jaisalmer Fort, which showcase the blend of military might and architectural grandeur characteristic of Rajput rule
